COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions, answered plainly.

How is my EMI calculated?

Your EMI is based on the principal, monthly interest rate and repayment tenure. The calculator gives an indicative result.

Does checking eligibility guarantee approval?

No. Eligibility results and rates are indicative. Final approval depends on verification and lender assessment.

What documents might I need?

Identity, address and income documents may be requested depending on the loan and assessment.
